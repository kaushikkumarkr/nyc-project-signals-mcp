# NYC Project Signals

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Python standard-library ingestion, SQLite, and a local web application. Implementation choice under the approved portable pipeline and searchable-demo plan. No package installation or cloud hosting is required to explore exported data.

## Users

NYC commercial cleaners, restaurant suppliers, signage and building-service businesses; later, agencies serving these customers.

## Product Purpose

Connect public records into evidence-backed project histories across all five boroughs. Help a business decide which projects warrant further research.

## Operating Context

Search and filter three feeds, inspect the source evidence and business roles, save projects locally, and export CSV files. Operator runs bounded incremental ingestion and reviews quality before offering subscriptions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The approved first release includes an exported database, repeatable update pipeline, searchable demo, CSV exports, and a measured quality report. Initial window: 90 days, with older linked records where needed. Azure credit maximum $700; ongoing target $100/month. Verify remaining balance, cutoff and eligible billing before paid requests. No claim that permits establish current buying intent. No fabricated contacts or dates. Payment-provider setup, source coverage, and measured demand remain launch gates.

## Evidence on Hand

Official NYC DOB NOW and legacy application/permit datasets, occupancy records, NY pending liquor licenses and PLUTO. No customer testimonials, revenue, human-reviewed accuracy metrics or comprehensive coverage claims yet.

## Product Principles

- Evidence precedes every factual claim.
- Keep projects, buildings, business roles and source events distinct.
- Show incomplete coverage and uncertain matches.
- Store portable artifacts and avoid recurring infrastructure commitments.
- Validate each feed before charging customers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, responsive layouts, visible focus, explicit labels and readable contrast.
